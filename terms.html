Last updated 22 November 2026. WhatHappened is in a test phase with invited users.

What this is

WhatHappened reads a chat export and describes patterns in it: who wrote, when, how fast and how the tone moved. It is a prototype and it is free during the test.

What this is not

  • It is not counselling, therapy or advice of any kind.
  • It cannot know what happened outside the chat: calls, time together, or what someone felt and did not write.
  • It cannot confirm or rule out that someone else was involved. Any sign it shows on that subject is only a pattern in messages.
  • Scores and labels are estimates and they can be wrong. Do not make important decisions based on them alone.

If a breakup is weighing on you heavily, talking to someone you trust or a professional will help more than a chart.

Your side

  • You must be 18 or over.
  • Use only chats you took part in.
  • The result describes another person too. Be careful about who you show it to, and do not use it to harass, expose or pressure anyone.
  • Do not try to break, overload or copy the service.

The deep reading

The deep reading is written by an AI model from your charts and a selection of messages. It can make mistakes or read too much into something. During the test each invitation link includes a limited number of relationships and deep readings.

Availability and liability

The service is provided as it is. It may change, stop or have errors, especially during the test. As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for decisions you make based on the results. Nothing here limits rights you have by law as a consumer.

Condiciones de uso

Qué es

WhatHappened lee un chat exportado y describe sus patrones: quién escribió, cuándo, qué tan rápido y cómo cambió el tono. Es un prototipo y es gratis durante la prueba.

Qué no es

  • No es terapia, consejería ni asesoría de ningún tipo.
  • No puede saber lo que pasó fuera del chat: llamadas, tiempo juntos, o lo que alguien sintió y no escribió.
  • No puede confirmar ni descartar que hubiera otra persona. Cualquier señal que muestre sobre ese tema es solo un patrón en los mensajes.
  • Los puntajes y etiquetas son estimaciones y pueden estar equivocados. No tomes decisiones importantes solo con ellos.

Si una ruptura te está pesando mucho, hablar con alguien de confianza o con un profesional te ayudará más que un gráfico.

  • Debes tener 18 años o más.
  • Usa solo chats en los que participaste.
